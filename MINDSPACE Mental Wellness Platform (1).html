Mental Health & Clinical Disclaimer

Please read this important notice regarding the scope and purpose of the assessments on MINDSPACE.

Educational & Self-Awareness Purpose Only: The questionnaires provided on MINDSPACE are designed exclusively for personal reflection, emotional self-monitoring, and psychoeducational awareness.

Not a Diagnostic Instrument: Results obtained from these assessments do not constitute a psychiatric diagnosis, medical evaluation, or treatment recommendation. They are not intended to replace consultation with a licensed psychologist, psychiatrist, or medical professional.

Configurable Thresholds: Category scores are derived from standard non-clinical scoring thresholds for illustrative self-evaluation.

100% Confidential & Local Privacy

Your privacy and emotional safety are paramount. MINDSPACE is designed to keep your responses completely private.

Zero Server Storage: Your assessment responses, total scores, and generated reports are stored strictly inside your own browser session using temporary client memory.

No Identity Tracking: We do not ask for your name, email address, IP address tracking, or personal identifiers.

No Third-Party Analytics: Mental health data is never packaged, sold, or transmitted to external advertisers or diagnostic networks.

How MINDSPACE Works

A thoughtful 3-step process to foster clarity, mindfulness, and personal growth.

1
Select an Assessment Choose from 5 domain-specific questionnaires focused on stress, anxiety, emotional balance, burnout, or self-esteem.
2
Reflect on Recent Experience Answer 10 carefully calibrated questions based on how you have felt over the past two weeks. Take your time without rush or timers.
3
Receive Practical Insights Explore an editorial summary with pattern identification, tailored actionable suggestions, and personal reflection prompts.
